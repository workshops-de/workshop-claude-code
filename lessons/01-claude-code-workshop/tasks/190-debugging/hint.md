<details>
<summary>💡 Hint 1: Two sentences, two clues</summary>

The ticket says two different things: one series is broken already, the others break only after
a save. A good hypothesis explains both sentences at once. If yours explains only one, keep
looking.

</details>

<details>
<summary>💡 Hint 2: Compare a good case with a bad one</summary>

Put a series that works next to one that doesn't and compare everything you can observe.
Differences you can name are evidence; "something is off" is not.

</details>

<details>
<summary>💡 Hint 3: Ask the history</summary>

Once you know which code is involved, ask when it last changed and why. A commit message often
tells you what the author tried to achieve, and what they didn't think of.

</details>

<details>
<summary>💡 Hint 4: Push back on the first fix</summary>

If Claude proposes a fix in the one place the ticket points at, ask: what else goes through
the same code? A fix that only hides the symptom will come back as the next ticket.

</details>
